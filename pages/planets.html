Planets of Our Solar System

Our solar system consists of eight planets, which are divided into two main categories: Terrestrial and Jovian.

Mercury

The smallest planet and closest to the Sun.

Venus

The hottest planet in our solar system.

Earth

Our home and the only planet known to harbor life.

Mars

The Red Planet, home to Olympus Mons.
