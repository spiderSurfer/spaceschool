Pioneers of the Void

Humanity's journey into the solar system began with brave explorers who left our atmosphere to touch the stars.

Yuri Gagarin

The first human to orbit the Earth in 1961, aboard Vostok 1.

Neil Armstrong

Commanded Apollo 11 and became the first person to walk on the Moon in 1969.

Valentina Tereshkova

The first woman in space, completing 48 orbits of Earth in 1963.

Mission Statistics

Over 600 people from 38 countries have traveled to space as of 2024. Most missions currently target the International Space Station (ISS), but upcoming Artemis missions aim for the Moon and Mars.
