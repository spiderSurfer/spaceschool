Frequently Asked Questions

What is Solar School?

Solar School is an interactive educational platform designed to teach students about astronomy and space exploration through gamified learning.

How do I earn Cosmic Coins?

You earn coins by achieving high scores in missions (Games) and completing Learning Module quizzes with 90% or higher accuracy.

Can I play offline?

Yes! Once you have visited a page once, our Service Worker caches the content, allowing you to learn and play even without an internet connection.

How do I share my progress?

Visit the Leaderboard and click "Share My Journey" to generate a custom mission card you can show to your friends.
